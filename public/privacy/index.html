URBAN PROCURES — PRIVACY POLICY

Urban Procures is operated in the UAE by Urban Fixperts Technical Services L.L.C. This notice explains what we collect when you use urbanprocures.com and the related desk services.

1. WHO WE ARE

2. WHAT WE COLLECT

2.1 Account data. Name, company, email, phone, trade licence details, and the role you choose (contractor or vendor).

2.2 RFQ and quote data. Project scope, site area or address, drawings, BOQs, quotations, and award decisions.

2.3 Technical data. Session cookies needed to keep you signed in. We do not run advertising or analytics cookies on the public site.

3. WHY WE USE IT

To create accounts, issue RFQs, invite vendors, compare quotations, complete awards, keep an audit trail, and contact you about your request.

4. IDENTITY FIREWALL

On public Get Quotes packs, contractor name, phone and email stay off the vendor pack until award. Site area may stay visible so vendors can price. After award, the winning vendor and the contractor receive each other’s introduction details. Other vendors receive a “not selected” notice only.

5. WHO WE SHARE WITH

Supabase (hosting and auth), Cloudflare (site and files), and Zoho (mail). We do not sell personal data. Vendors see only what the award rules allow.

6. RETENTION

Account, RFQ, quote and award records are kept for the life of the platform and any period required for UAE commercial or tax records.
