Frequently asked questions

Answers before you register or post.

A quick guide for clients, contractors, vendors and suppliers using Urban Procures for UAE construction procurement.

Clients and contractors

Free access
Is it free for clients?

Yes. Clients and contractors can register, post RFQs, compare quotations and award work without a platform fee.

Can I post without an account?

Individuals can submit a public quote request. Registered client workspaces are better for repeat RFQs, tracking quotations and award history.

Are my details shown to vendors?

No. The admin desk reviews and prepares the work pack so vendors quote on the scope while your identity stays protected until award.

Vendors and suppliers

Verified profiles
How does vendor approval work?

Register your company, verify your email, then upload your trade licence from the vendor workspace. The admin desk reviews it before approval.

When can I quote?

Verified vendors can receive matched RFQ invitations and submit quotations with optional quotation documents.

What happens after an award?

Awarded parties receive the released contact details needed to proceed, while the platform keeps a record of the procurement decision.

Documents, support and trust

Desk reviewed
Can documents be uploaded?

Yes. Clients can attach supporting RFQ documents, vendors can upload licences and quotation documents, and the admin desk can open submitted files for review.

Where do I get help?

Email desk@urbanprocures.com with your registered email, company name and RFQ reference if available.

Why no public contact swapping?

Identity protection keeps quotations fair, reduces direct bypassing and gives both sides a clearer audit trail.
