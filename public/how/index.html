For Manpower Supply

AED 1/hr/labour

Special pricing for manpower contracts:

  • Platform fee: AED 1 per hour per labourer
  • Calculated for the full contract duration
  • PDC cheque required (dated same as contractor payment)
  • Example: 10 workers × 3 months (720 hrs) = AED 7,200 platform fee

Identity Firewall

During the bidding process, vendor names are masked. Contractors see bids without knowing who submitted them. After an award is made, names are revealed on the homepage as social proof.

This protects the platform's revenue model and ensures fair competition among vendors.
