About Urban Procures

The digital RFQ desk for construction work in the UAE.

Urban Procures lets clients post construction, fit-out, MEP and manpower work once, and receive structured quotations from verified contractors. Names stay off the pack until an award.

The site is operated in Dubai by Urban Fixperts Technical Services L.L.C. (DED licence 1563683). Invoices and contracts after award are issued by that licensed entity — not by a separate marketplace company.

Registration is free. A platform fee is recorded only if a contractor is awarded the work. Fee details live in the vendor terms at sign-up.
