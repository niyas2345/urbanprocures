# Legal/commercial requirements supplied by project owner — 2026-08-28

These requirements supersede earlier working assumptions for implementation. They are product requirements, not legal advice, and must be reviewed by qualified UAE counsel before production use.

## Service fee
- Construction, Civil, MEP, Fit-Out and related works: AED 500 OR 2.5% of Award Value, whichever is higher.
- Award Value is evidenced by the applicable LPO, Purchase Order, Work Order, contract, written award confirmation or accepted documentation.
- VAT is added where legally applicable.
- Manpower/workforce fee follows the hourly commercial terms in the relevant RFQ/quotation/agreement.
- Current agreed LPO trigger wording: fee becomes due when the Client issues an LPO, Purchase Order, Work Order or equivalent written order for the relevant scope.
- Fee is calculated on the full order value, not each instalment. Where payment is staged, the full fee becomes payable at the first payment stage unless otherwise expressly agreed in writing.
- Multiple LPOs/POs/WOs for the same RFQ or substantially related scope may be separate fee-triggering transactions.
- Vendor must provide order documentation upon request for fee verification.

## Platform model
- Urban Procure is operated by URBAN FIXPERTS TECHNICAL SERVICES L.L.C.
- The platform may facilitate procurement, RFQs, sourcing, communication and comparison.
- Unless separately agreed, Urban Procure does not become the contractor, subcontractor, supplier or employer responsible for execution.
- Vendors and clients may use identity protection during pre-award procurement.
- Where a physical site inspection, measurement, technical assessment or other direct interaction is genuinely required to prepare a quotation, minimum necessary information may be disclosed. This does not constitute a general waiver of confidentiality or identity protection.
- Any disclosed information is restricted to the relevant RFQ/site inspection/quotation/evaluation/execution and may not be used to circumvent Urban Procure or avoid applicable fees.
